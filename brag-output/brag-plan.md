# Mosaic Forge — `/brag` Launch Video & 10-Minute Narrated Master Showcase Plan

## 1. Product Inspection & Rubric Answers

1. **What is it (one sentence)?**
   **Mosaic Forge** is a 100% local-first, zero-subscription creative suite, separate-ISO broadcast recording studio, multi-track video editor, GarageBand & FL Studio DAW workstation, and parametric 3D/2D CAD workspace running natively in the browser via Rust/WebAssembly, WebAudio, and WebCodecs.
2. **Who is it for, and what does it do for them?**
   Engineers, filmmakers, podcasters, musicians, and designers who want an integrated desktop-grade studio where 3D STEP/STL CAD models, separate screen & camera ISO recordings, multi-track video edits, GarageBand/FL Studio audio stems, and layered raster/vector/PDF/office documents live in one local IndexedDB + OPFS project hub.
3. **What sets it apart?**
   - **18 Live Embedded Studios** (14 Rust/WASM editors + RiverCast & OneStudio Separate ISO Studio + Studio Cut Multi-Track NLE + BandForge GarageBand DAW + FruityForge 21 FL Studio DAW).
   - **True Separate ISO Track Recording**: Screen capture (`screen-recording-iso.webm`), camera video (`camera-video-iso.webm`), and 48kHz PCM audio (`mic-audio-iso.wav`) record on independent parallel streams so the webcam is never burned over the screen recording.
   - **Cross-Workspace Handoff**: Extrude a 3D motor plate in SolidCraft, draft its 2D DXF in CADCraft, record a screen/camera walkthrough in RiverCast, re-frame the PIP in Studio Cut, score the soundtrack in BandForge & FruityForge 21, and publish the spec sheet in PdfCraft/WordCraft/GridCraft.
4. **What's the most impressive claim?**
   Zero cloud accounts, zero subscriptions, 18 live studios, and real manufacturing/production exports (`.STEP`, `.STL`, `.DXF`, separate `.WEBM` ISO tracks, `.OTIO`, 48kHz `.WAV` stems, `.PSD`, `.SVG`, `.PDF`, `.DOCX`, `.XLSX`, `.PPTX`).
5. **What's the visual hook?**
   A rapid kinetic split-screen showing a 3D parametric NEMA-17 motor plate extruding in SolidCraft while RiverCast records separate Screen + Camera ISO streams and FruityForge 21 lights up its 16-step 808 sequencer.
6. **What real UI or flow should be shown?**
   Every single one of the 18 workspaces and 5 shell views in action, using real browser recordings and high-DPI captures of us operating each tool, paired with kinetic motion-graphics callouts, animated cursor/highlight rings, live waveform/progress HUDs, and multi-persona OpenRouter neural voiceover.
7. **What tone fits?**
   `cinematic` + `tech_keynote` (punchy, high-craft engineering authority with playful confidence).
8. **What's the one-line share caption?**
   We built Mosaic Forge: 18 local-first creative, broadcast ISO, DAW, and 3D CAD studios running in one browser tab—with separate screen & camera ISO exports, GarageBand & FL Studio workstations, and parametric STEP/STL CAD.
